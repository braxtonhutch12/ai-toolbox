Which should you start with?

Start with the tool that solves the first bottleneck in your business. A content-driven business may benefit most from a content/SEO tool and Canva. An ecommerce business needs a storefront first. Add automation after you have repetitive tasks worth automating. Paying for every tool on day one usually creates expense before it creates value.

A simple beginner setup

A practical approach is to choose one platform for the business itself, one tool for creating marketing assets, and then add specialized content or automation software only when the workload justifies it. AI can speed up work, but review generated copy, images, recommendations and automated actions before publishing or sending them to customers.
