What Is Kitful?

Kitful is an AI content and SEO platform built around a continuous workflow: research what people are searching for, create content, publish it, monitor performance and improve pages that begin losing search visibility.

When you add a website, Kitful can analyze the site's existing content to learn about the business, target audience and writing style. That information can then be used when creating future articles.

Key Kitful Features

1. SEO Topic Research

Kitful can research potential content opportunities using live search information and the content already published on your website. This can help website owners decide what topics to cover instead of simply guessing what to write next.

2. AI Article Creation

The platform can create several types of content, including guides, how-to articles, listicles and comparison articles. Kitful also supports turning YouTube content into blog posts.

3. Brand Voice

Kitful can learn a writing style from existing published posts, a URL or a file. This is intended to make generated content sound more consistent with the rest of a website instead of producing the same generic AI tone for every business.

4. Internal Linking

Internal links are important for both readers and site structure. Kitful can identify pages already on your website and place relevant internal links into generated articles.

5. AI Images

Kitful can also generate images for articles. Multiple image styles are available, allowing publishers to keep a more consistent visual style across their content.

6. Publishing and Scheduling

Kitful supports direct publishing integrations with WordPress, Ghost, Shopify and Framer, while webhooks can be used for additional workflows. Articles can also be reviewed before publication.

7. Google Search Console Optimization

One of Kitful's more interesting features is its optimization workflow. Instead of focusing only on new content, it can use Google Search Console information to help identify existing pages that may be slipping in search performance.

Kitful Pricing

At the time of this review, Kitful's Starter plan costs $39 per month and includes 250 credits, support for one website, and up to 10 articles.

Kitful says writing a full article generally costs 20 credits, while generated images require additional credits. Actual monthly usage therefore depends on the type of content and number of images you create.

Pricing and plan limits can change, so check Kitful directly for the latest information before subscribing.

Who Is Kitful Best For?

Kitful makes the most sense for people who want to build organic search traffic through consistent content, including:

  • Bloggers
  • Small businesses

Kitful becomes more interesting when you want one system handling more of the complete SEO content workflow rather than using separate tools for research, writing, images, internal linking and optimization.

Is Kitful Worth It?

Whether Kitful is worth paying for depends mainly on how much content you plan to publish. At $39 per month for the Starter plan, someone publishing consistently can potentially get considerably more value from the platform than someone who only writes a few articles each year.

The main reason to consider Kitful isn't simply that it can write with AI. Plenty of tools can do that. Its stronger differentiator is connecting research, writing, site context, internal linking, publishing and ongoing optimization in one workflow.

Our Bottom Line

Kitful is a promising option for website owners who want AI to handle more of the repetitive work involved in SEO content creation while still keeping control over what gets published.

We especially like the combination of topic research, brand voice, internal linking and Search Console-based optimization. However, every AI-generated article should still be reviewed for accuracy, usefulness and originality before it goes live.
