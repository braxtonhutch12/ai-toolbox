What is Writesonic?

Writesonic currently positions itself as an AI search visibility and content/SEO platform. Starter includes tracking brand visibility across ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, an AI Article Writer, content strategy features and site audits. It currently lists 15 AI articles per month and one user on Starter.

What is Jasper?

Jasper is positioned as an AI platform built for marketing. Pro includes one seat, Canvas, marketing agents, Brand Voice customization, knowledge assets and audience features. Business expands into custom agents, broader customization, governance, API access and advanced workflows.

Pricing in 2026

Writesonic currently lists Starter at $79 per month billed annually. Jasper lists Pro at $59 per month billed annually or $69 billed monthly. The products include different capabilities and limits, so price alone is not an apples-to-apples comparison.

Where Writesonic stands out

Writesonic combines content creation with SEO and AI-search visibility. That combination is particularly relevant for businesses that want to publish content while tracking how their brand appears in AI-generated answers.

Where Jasper stands out

Jasper is strongly centered on marketing workflows and brand consistency. Its published Pro features include Brand Voices, knowledge assets, audiences, Canvas and marketing agents, while Business adds more customization and team controls.

Which should you try?

Match the platform to the job. If your priority is SEO content plus AI-search visibility, Writesonic's feature set is especially relevant. If your priority is brand-controlled marketing creation and workflows, Jasper's product structure is worth comparing closely. Test either tool on a real business task before paying.
