Best AI tools for small businesses in 2026

A practical shortlist for marketing, automation, design and customer workflows.

Marketing & content

1. Writesonic

Best for: businesses investing in content, SEO and visibility in AI search.

Writesonic's current platform combines content creation with tools focused on traditional search and AI-search visibility. Its content tools cover formats such as blog posts, sales emails, ads, newsletters and product pages.

Watch for: If you only need occasional writing, compare the current plan limits with how much content you actually publish.

Automation

2. Zapier

Best for: connecting apps and automating repetitive work.

Zapier uses trigger-and-action workflows to move work between apps and can add AI steps and agents. Its current documentation says it supports more than 9,000 apps.

Good uses: lead routing, onboarding, notifications, form processing and summarization.

Design

3. Canva

Best for: social graphics, presentations, ads, videos and branded content.

Canva Business combines templates, brand controls and AI-assisted creation. Canva's Magic Studio includes writing, design, image-editing and media-generation tools.

CRM, sales & marketing

4. HubSpot Breeze

Best for: businesses using HubSpot for customer, sales and marketing workflows.

Breeze Assistant can generate content, summarize CRM records, help prepare for meetings and complete tasks inside HubSpot. HubSpot also supports AI-assisted drafting and editing in its content tools.

Visit HubSpot

How to choose without wasting money

Start with the job that costs you the most time or directly affects revenue. Test one or two tools and measure whether they save meaningful time or improve output before adding more subscriptions.
