AI Toolbox Editorial Policy

AI Toolbox publishes practical guides, reviews and comparisons designed to help readers understand AI tools and choose software that fits their needs.

How We Research Our Content

We research information using sources such as official product websites, documentation, announcements and other reliable sources when appropriate.

Because AI products change quickly, features, pricing and availability may change after an article is published. We aim to update our content when important changes become known.

How We Evaluate AI Tools

When discussing or comparing tools, we may consider factors such as features, ease of use, intended audience, pricing, integrations and practical use cases.

We do not claim to personally test a product unless we have actually done so. When an article is based primarily on research and publicly available information, we aim to make that clear.

Accuracy and Updates

Our goal is to provide useful and accurate information. However, readers should verify important details such as current pricing, features and terms directly with the product provider before making a purchase or business decision.

Affiliate Links

Some links on AI Toolbox may be affiliate links. If a reader purchases a product through one of these links, AI Toolbox may receive a commission at no additional cost to the reader.

Affiliate relationships do not guarantee positive coverage. Our goal is to provide useful information regardless of whether a product offers an affiliate program.

AI-Assisted Content

AI tools may be used to assist with research, drafting, organization and editing. Content is reviewed before publication with the goal of making it useful, clear and accurate.

Corrections

If you notice information that appears incorrect or outdated, please contact us so we can review it.
