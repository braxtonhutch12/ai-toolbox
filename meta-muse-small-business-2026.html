Meta Muse for Small Business: What It Does, Pricing & How to Use It

Meta has introduced Muse for Small Business, expanding its new AI agent with tools designed specifically to help business owners save time, understand their business and find new customers.

Unlike a traditional AI chatbot that mainly answers questions, Muse is designed to actually complete tasks using the business tools you already use.

Quick answer: Meta Muse can connect with business services such as Shopify, QuickBooks, Stripe, Canva, Slack, Facebook and Instagram. It can analyze business information, help create marketing campaigns, review performance and assist with everyday business tasks.

What Is Meta Muse?

Muse is Meta's AI agent. Meta first introduced the personal version of Muse in September 2026 and later expanded it with capabilities aimed at small businesses.

The major difference between an AI agent and a basic chatbot is that an agent can work across connected services and take actions toward a goal instead of simply generating an answer.

For example, a business owner could ask Muse to analyze sales and marketing performance and help build a growth plan.

What Can Muse Do for a Small Business?

Meta says Muse can help business owners with tasks including:

  • Analyzing sales and business performance
  • Reviewing marketing campaigns
  • Finding opportunities to improve advertising
  • Drafting new marketing campaigns
  • Reviewing business expenses
  • Helping manage emails and other work
  • Analyzing social media performance
  • Suggesting ideas for growing the business

How Could a Small Business Actually Use Muse?

Imagine you run an online store.

Instead of separately checking Shopify sales, Instagram performance, advertising results and financial information, you could connect supported services to Muse and ask it to analyze what is happening across your business.

You might ask:

“Look at my recent sales and marketing performance. What appears to be working, what isn't working, and what should I focus on next month?”

The ability to work with information across several business tools is one of the most interesting parts of AI agents like Muse.

Does Muse Automatically Publish or Spend Money?

Meta says users remain in control of important actions. According to the company, Muse requires approval before something is published, sent or money is spent.

Business owners should still review permissions carefully before connecting financial, customer or business accounts to any AI service.

How Much Does Meta Muse Cost?

Meta says Muse is free for most everyday usage and that subscription plans are available for people who need more usage.

Pricing and plan details may change as the product develops, so users should check Meta's current Muse information before choosing a paid plan.

Who Should Consider Trying Muse?

Muse could be particularly interesting for:

  • Small business owners
  • Online store owners
  • Solo entrepreneurs
  • Marketing teams
  • Creators running businesses
  • People managing several business apps

The biggest potential advantage is reducing the amount of time spent jumping between different business tools.

Is Meta Muse Worth Trying?

Muse is very new, so it is too early to know how useful it will ultimately become for every business.

However, the ability to connect an AI agent directly with tools such as Shopify, QuickBooks, Stripe, Canva, Facebook and Instagram makes it worth watching for businesses already using those platforms.

The best approach is to start with low-risk tasks, review what Muse produces and gradually decide whether giving it access to additional business tools actually saves you time.

Meta Muse vs Traditional AI Chatbots

Traditional AI chatbots are excellent for brainstorming, writing and answering questions. AI agents are increasingly designed to go further by interacting with connected tools and completing multi-step tasks.

That distinction could become increasingly important as businesses adopt AI.
