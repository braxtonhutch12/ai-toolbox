Shopify Canvas: How to Build an Online Store With AI in 2026

Shopify has introduced Canvas, a new AI-powered design workspace that could make building and customizing an online store much easier.

Instead of relying only on menus, settings and manual theme changes, merchants can work with Shopify's AI assistant, Sidekick, to help turn ideas into changes across their store.

Quick answer: Shopify Canvas is a visual workspace where merchants can view their store pages together and use Sidekick to help design and customize them.

What is Shopify Canvas?

Canvas is Shopify's new design surface for online stores. It lets merchants see multiple pages together, zoom into individual parts of the store and make changes with Sidekick.

Shopify says Sidekick can work directly with theme files and make coordinated changes across a store rather than only editing one small element at a time.

How does Shopify Canvas work?

Merchants can work visually inside Canvas or describe changes to Sidekick through chat.

For example, a business owner could ask Sidekick to change the direction of a homepage design or adjust elements across several pages. Changes can then appear inside Canvas so the merchant can review and refine them.

Do you need to know how to code?

One of the biggest potential advantages of Canvas is that it is designed to make custom store design more accessible to people without extensive coding or design experience.

Sidekick can work with the code behind a Shopify theme while the merchant focuses more on describing and reviewing the result.

Can Canvas build an entire Shopify store?

Canvas is designed to handle much larger design tasks than a basic AI text generator. Shopify says Sidekick can work across theme templates and files and can take on tasks ranging from individual section changes to broader store redesigns.

However, Canvas is still new. Shopify says it is not replacing the existing store editor yet, and the company expects the product to continue changing.

Who should consider Shopify Canvas?

Canvas could be especially useful for entrepreneurs, small businesses and online sellers who want a more customized Shopify store without doing every design or coding task manually.

It may also make it easier to experiment with different layouts and ideas before settling on a final store design.

Is Shopify Canvas available now?

Shopify announced Canvas on October 1, 2026 and said it would roll out to merchants over the following days. Availability may therefore vary by store while the rollout continues.

Is Shopify Canvas worth trying?

If you already use Shopify and have access to Canvas, it may be worth experimenting with—particularly if store design or theme customization has been difficult or time-consuming for you.

Because Canvas is still early, merchants should review AI-generated changes carefully before publishing them to a live store.
